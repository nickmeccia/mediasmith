Two structural things fall out of this layout, both useful to know:

- The top-left cell is a space (empty pixel) and the bottom-right is █ (full block) — the two extremes sit on the diagonal.
- Every character showing as a plain block/quadrant (▖ ▗ ▘ ▚ ▝ ▞ ▀ ▄ ▌ ▐ ▙ ▟ ▛ ▜ █ and the 4 quarter-bars ▂ ▆ 🮂 🮅) is a borrowed glyph, not an octant. Those are the 26 that predate the octant block.

If a glyph in the grid renders as tofu, this tells you which font coverage you need:

- 230 — Block Octants · U+1CD00–U+1CDE5 · the new Unicode 16.0 block, names BLOCK OCTANT-<cells>.
- 16 — Block Elements / quadrants · U+0020 (space), U+2580 2584 258C 2590 2588 (halves + full), U+2596–259F (the ten quadrants). The ·, halves, and quadrant glyphs in the grid.
- 6 — half-width quarter blocks · U+1CEA0 1CEA3 1CEA8 1CEAB (LEFT/RIGHT HALF UPPER/LOWER ONE QUARTER = single corner pixels) and U+1FBE6 1FBE7 (MIDDLE LEFT/RIGHT ONE QUARTER = the vertical-center dominoes, values 20 & 40 — the hardest two to locate).
- 4 — quarter-height bars · U+1FB82 🮂 U+1FB85 🮅 (upper ¼, upper ¾) and U+2582 ▂ U+2586 ▆ (lower ¼, lower ¾).

Caveat worth flagging: the 230 octants are new (Sept 2024), so most fonts won't have them. For a reference that actually renders, use GNU Unifont, Cascadia Code ≥ 2404, Julia Mono, or Iosevka — those ship the full Block Octants range.
